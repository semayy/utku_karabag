Section 01

The Brand.

Foreword

Hello!
Welcome to the
Elif Tarman Studio guidelines.

In these guidelines, you will find the architectural blueprint for our visual brand identity. It is critical to read these rules carefully to understand how we shape the space in which we communicate.

By adhering strictly to these structural guidelines, we engineer a monolithic, recognizable, and uniform aesthetic for our high-end clientele.

About Us

Elif Tarman Studio is one of the world's preeminent interior architecture firms. Since our founding, we have advanced a global mission to discover and engineer spaces of extreme serenity and monumental proportions. We do not decorate; we manipulate volume, light, and mass.

02

Section 02

Brand Values.

The Inherent Worth of Void

A relationship to empty space is beneficial to the human psyche. We believe true luxury is found in silence, restraint, and the deliberate absence of ornamental clutter. The void allows the architecture to speak.

Material Truth

We use honest, enduring materials. Concrete must feel like concrete; raw steel must be allowed to patinate. We build spaces that serve as physical anchors in an increasingly digital world.

Intergenerational Permanence

We do not design for trends. We engineer spaces that will age beautifully over decades, inheriting character through time and wear.

Architectural Chiaroscuro

Light is our primary material. We manipulate harsh natural light and deep shadow to carve out volumes and define the haptic textures of our environments.

The Studio.

Manifesto & Mission

Foreword

We do not decorate.
We engineer void, light, and mass.

Elif Tarman Studio is an interior architecture practice defined by monolithic discipline, brutal restraint, and the timeless manipulation of space.

Our visual brand guidelines are not merely design recommendations—they are an architectural blueprint. The typography, grid coordinates, chromatic limits, and material selections documented here maintain our monolithic identity across all physical and digital expressions worldwide.

Core Positioning

"To strip away the superfluous until only spatial truth, architectural gravity, and emotional permanence remain."

Standard Image Treatment:

• Desaturation: 100% (Grayscale)
• Contrast Curve: +35% to +45% (Chiaroscuro)
• Highlights: Pure Paper (#F7F6F2)

Section 01 // Spatial Creed Studio Manifesto Plate 04 / 36
Foundational Creed

We do not decorate.
We engineer void, light, and mass.

Elif Tarman Studio is an interior architecture practice defined by monolithic discipline, brutal restraint, and the timeless manipulation of volume.

Our visual brand identity is an extension of our architectural projects. It does not entertain decorative vanity; instead, it establishes structural harmony between solid surfaces and vast negative space.

Core Proposition

"Strip away the superfluous until only spatial truth, architectural gravity, and emotional permanence remain."

Section 02 // Core Ethics 4 Foundational Pillars Plate 05 / 36

Pillars of Practice

Architectural Ethics.

Pillar 01

The Inherent Worth of Void

Emptiness is not absence; it is active presence. True spatial luxury is discovered in silence, strict reduction, and deliberate exclusion of ornamental noise.

Pillar 02

Material Authenticity

Materials must remain raw and honest. Board-formed concrete must feel cold; porous travertine must breathe; unlacquered steel must patinate gracefully.

Pillar 03

Decadal Permanence

We oppose ephemeral, disposable trends. We engineer volumes and graphic artifacts built to age with dignity over decades of sunlight exposure.

Pillar 04

Architectural Chiaroscuro

Light is a physical sculpting tool. We balance harsh, directional sunlight with cavernous shadow to carve out monumental volumes.

Ethical Framework Zero Ornamentation Plate 05 / 36

8pt Baseline Grid

Vertical Rhythm.

All body copy, margins, and section paddings are integer multiples of an **8px baseline unit** (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 120px). This guarantees strict vertical cadence.
